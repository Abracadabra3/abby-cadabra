Have you seen the symbols at the top of the page before? Maybe wondering what they mean? Well, this page is here to answer that question!
The first symbol is a heart. The heart stands for love because God loves you. God made you, and He loves you. God's love is an unconditional, never changing, forever love and nothing you do will change the fact that God loves you.
The next symbol is a division symbol, because sin divides. Sin is kind of a church word, but what it means is disobeying God. If God says do and you don't, that's sin. If God says don't and you do it anyway, that's also sin. Sin separates us from God, because God is perfect and sin and perfection can't go together. Even though we are separated from God, he still loves us and nothing can change that.

The third symbol is a cross because Jesus Christ, who is both God and human, makes a way for us to be with God again. Jesus is perfect because He is God, and he died as a human to pay the price for our sins. Bur Jesus didn't stay dead for long. Three days later, Jesus rose from the dead and appeared to many people.

Because Jesus paid the price for our sins, He can offer something no one else can: forgiveness of sins. Sin is what separates us from God, and removing sin is how we can get back to a right relationship with God again.

The last symbol is a question mark because the most important question ever is this: Now that you know this, what will you do?

Will you accept Jesus's free gift of forgiveness from sins, a right relationship with God, and spending eternity with Him?

Or wil you ignore the gift and not have this relationship with God?

The choice is yours.
